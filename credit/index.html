    What changed

    The last 30 days for the tenants in view: rating actions, results that came in, and net leverage moves of 0.3x or more against the prior period.

    Screener

    One row per tenant in the current filter. Click a column to sort it and a name to open that tenant's detail. Leverage is the latest stored figure; coverage, margin and dividend cover are the latest fiscal year.

    Where each tenant sits on the rating scale

    Latest S&P, Moody's and Fitch ratings, best rated first. The shaded bar spans the split between agencies. Hover or tab to a marker for the action and date.

      Credit trends

      Annual net leverage, interest coverage, EBITDA margin and capex intensity since 2021, for the tenants picked in Compare a metric. Hover a line or a name to pick one tenant out. Basis follows the toggle above.

      Leverage vs rating

      Latest net leverage against the average of each tenant's agency ratings. Further right means more debt per unit of EBITDA; higher means a better rating. Tenants sitting low and left look strong for their rating, high and right look stretched.

      Free cash flow after dividends

      Operating cash flow minus capex minus dividends paid, by fiscal year, in each tenant's own currency. Red bars are years where dividends weren't covered by cash left after capex.

      Revenue mix

      How each tenant's revenue splits, using the labels the company reports. Nested splits (Equinix services within a region, Vodafone Business within a market, DT customer groups within Germany) sit under their parent.
